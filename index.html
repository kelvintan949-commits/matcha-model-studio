About this model ↗

Source: Cafe_Matcha_Wall_Finishes, {3D}. All 220 physical elements visible in that view are included, with original mesh geometry, surface assignments and eight referenced texture maps.

Warm architectural daylight, soft cast shadows and fine surface relief follow the supplied realism reference. Desktop modes add depth-aware contact shading. Materials are translated to browser PBR; lighting, reflectivity and procedural shading can differ from Revit. The folding animation is a prototype between native open/closed poses. The labeled AC is an added concept unit. Controls simulate operation in this webpage. Walk mode uses simplified shell and furniture collision. Auto quality scales resolution, shadows and a shared light budget for your device; all original geometry stays available.

Interior view automatically hides camera-facing walls as you orbit, revealing the interior and the walls behind it.

WELCOME TO MODEL STUDIO

Step inside your model.

Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, and right-drag to pan. Click any object to inspect it. On touch screens, use two fingers to pan.

  1. Walk inside. Enter first person at eye level. Use W A S D to walk and F (or the Free look button) to capture the mouse for unlimited turning. Escape or F releases the mouse. If your browser blocks capture, a message appears and free look stays off. Click an item while looking to operate it. On mobile, hold the arrow controls and drag the model to look. Return with Axonometric.
  2. Explore the space. Interior view hides camera-facing walls as you orbit and restores them when they move behind the interior. Wall finishes and the entrance follow their host wall. The ceiling starts hidden. Assembled restores the complete model.
  3. Try the details. Open the main door, switch lights or AC, and pull out a chair. Use the inspector for individual controls.
  4. See how it comes together. Use the explosion slider to separate floors, walls and ceiling layers.
  5. Inspect anything. Browse all objects, focus, isolate or hide them. Press Escape to clear selection or isolation.

AC airflow and lighting are visual simulations. Overall dimensions describe the model envelope in meters.
